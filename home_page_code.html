Mission, Vision & Values

🎯

Mission

To empower students in rural areas by providing high-quality STEM education, enabling them to shape their future through technology.

👁️

Vision

A future where every student, regardless of background, has access to world-class technology and coding skills to innovate locally.

❤️

Values

Excellence, innovation, inclusivity, and community development. We believe in building resilient and confident young leaders.

The Problem

0%

of students in rural Shamva High Schools know a programming language.

We recognize the gap in technical skills and internet access in rural areas. We are stepping up to provide the much-needed infrastructure.

Our Solution

A 6-week fast-track program coding students from Shamva with basic computer, data, and logic skills.

The 6-Week Journey

Available in our free growing series.

PHASE 1 & 2

Weeks 1-2: Foundations

Introduction to basic programming syntax, data structures, and computer literacy.

PHASE 3

Week 3: Interactive UI

Building user interfaces using standard web technologies and understanding user experience.

PHASE 4 & 5

Weeks 4-5: AI & APIs

Integrating AI tools and external APIs to build robust, scalable applications.

PHASE 6

Week 6: Final Projects

Students conceptualize, build, and present their final projects to community stakeholders.
