person_book About Me

Welcome! I am Dr. Sarah Vance, a passionate educator with over 15 years of experience in facilitating deep literary analysis and fostering critical thinking among secondary and collegiate level students. My academic journey began at the University of Chicago, where I completed my doctorate focusing on 19th-century transatlantic literature.

Teaching Philosophy

I firmly believe that literature is not merely text on a page, but a living breathing dialogue between the author, the reader, and the historical context. My classroom operates as an enabling environment—highly structured to reduce cognitive friction, yet open enough to encourage rigorous debate.

  • Inquiry-Based Learning: Encouraging students to ask the 'why' and 'how' behind narratives.
  • Data-Informed Pedagogy: Utilizing formative assessments to adapt pacing and material relevance dynamically.
  • Inclusive Canons: Actively expanding reading lists to reflect diverse, global voices.

Current Projects

Currently, I am spearheading the departmental transition to a fully integrated digital portfolio system for graduating seniors, aiming to provide a holistic view of student progress beyond standardized metrics.
