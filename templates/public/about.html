About the Program

The Dev Academy AI & Coding Program was created to give middle school students in Zimbabwe hands-on experience with artificial intelligence and software development. Students learn by doing — building projects that matter to their communities.

Over the course of the program, students progress from foundational coding concepts through to machine learning basics, culminating in a final community-impact project they design, build, and present publicly.

Our methodology combines structured weekly coursework, collaborative assignments, teacher mentorship, and peer learning in a supportive cohort environment.
