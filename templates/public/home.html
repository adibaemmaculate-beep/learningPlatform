Shamva Innovators

Students in Shamva learn Python and AI by building software for problems they know firsthand. In six weeks, they go from writing their first lines of code to publishing a working application.

Mission, vision, and values

Mission

We teach students in Shamva the foundations of AI engineering through practical lessons based on problems in their community.

Vision

We are working toward an independent, locally led organization where graduates mentor new students and use technology to address community problems.

Values

We value curiosity, hands-on work, and service to the community. Students learn by making tools that matter to their families and neighbors.

Our course

Our six-week course takes students from their first line of Python to a published web application that uses AI.

The six-week course

From the first Python lesson to a published final project

PHASE ONE

Weeks 1-2: Python basics

Students learn Python syntax, data structures, and how to break a problem into steps a computer can follow.

PHASE TWO

Week 3: Web applications

Students use Streamlit to turn their Python scripts into simple web applications that other people can use.

PHASE THREE

Weeks 4-5: AI and APIs

Students learn how AI models work and connect AI APIs and outside data to their applications.

PHASE FOUR

Week 6: Final projects

Students finish and publish applications based on problems they have identified in their community. They present their work at a community showcase.
