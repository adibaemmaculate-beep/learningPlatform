Mission, Vision & Values

Mission

To teach students in Shamva the foundations of AI engineering in a way that is accessible, practical, and directly connected to the problems they see in their lives.

Vision

An independent, self-sustaining organization where graduates mentor new students, solving community problems through tech innovation.

Values

Curiosity over memorization. Building over lecturing. We believe students learn fastest when they build matters to their families and community.

The Problem

0%

of students surveyed at Wadzanai High School knew a programming language.

13.7%

Community challenges like drug abuse, water shortages, and unemployment persist while young people lack the tools to respond effectively.

Our Solution

A six-week, live-taught program taking students from their first line of code to a deployed AI-powered application.

The 6-Week Journey

Accelerated path to engineering impact

PHASE ONE

Weeks 1-2: Foundations

Mastering Python syntax, data structures, and the logic required to build computational solutions.

PHASE TWO

Week 3: Interactive UI

Deploying Streamlit to transform static scripts into dynamic web interfaces people can actually use.

PHASE THREE

Weeks 4-5: AI & APIs

Integrating Large Language Models and external data sources to power intelligent features.

PHASE FOUR

Week 6: Final Projects

The culmination: Students launch community-focused apps that address real Zimbabwean challenges.
